02 — Rules & Regulations

The Code

Non-negotiable. Every member agrees to this on entry. Break it, and the clan votes on what happens next.

I

Respect the tag

Never bring shame to { JSR } in public matches, servers, or chats. What you do under the tag is done in the name of everyone wearing it.

II

No member left behind

If a clanmate needs backup, you show up. Loyalty isn't optional here — it's the whole point of being a clan instead of a crowd.

III

Zero tolerance for disrespect

No harassment, discrimination, or targeting of other members or outsiders. Disagreements happen — cruelty doesn't get a pass.

IV

Stay active or step aside

Inactive without notice for 30 days? Your spot opens up. Life happens — just tell leadership before you disappear.

V

Leadership calls are final

Disputes go to the officers. Once a ruling is made, it stands. Take it up privately, never air it publicly.

VI

No leaking clan business

Internal strategy, disputes, and member matters stay internal. What's said in the clan stays in the clan.
